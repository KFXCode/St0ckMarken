📗 New to options? The plain-English guide
What you're buying. A call is like a coupon: the right to buy 100 shares at a set price (the strike) until a set date (the expiration). A put is the same but it wins when the stock FALLS. The coupon's price is the premium: premium $0.82 = one contract costs $82 (×100). You never have to buy the shares — almost everyone just sells the coupon once it's worth more.
Turn on options first (one time). In your broker: Settings → Options → Enable (Robinhood, Webull) or apply for options approval (Fidelity, Schwab). Level 1–2 approval is all you need to buy calls and puts.
⚠️ It does NOT sell itself. If the premium rises, the profit is only yours when you tap Sell to Close. Every day an option loses a little value (time decay); at expiration an out-of-the-money option becomes worthless — you lose the premium. Sell it yourself before expiry week.
Simple exit plan. Decide before you buy: take profit at +50% to +100%; cut losses at −50%; and be out at least a week before expiration.
⏰ What time should I trade? (super important!)
All times are Eastern Time (ET) — that's the clock the stock market uses. If you're on the West Coast, that's 3 hours EARLIER for you (10 AM ET = 7 AM your time).
📈 Stocks & options — Monday to Friday only:
• Market opens 9:30 AM ET, closes 4:00 PM ET.
• 6–9 AM ET: the app updates at 6 AM. Read the picks, no rush — the market's still closed.
• Skip 9:30–9:45 AM. The first 15 minutes are crazy jumpy and you often overpay.
• ⭐ BEST time to buy: 9:45–10:30 AM ET. Prices calm down and get fair. If you only trade once, 10:00 AM ET is the sweet spot — set a phone alarm!
• Right after you buy, set the sell-limit the app gives you. Then you're done for the day.
🪙 Crypto — any time, 7 days a week. It never closes! But calm times (mornings) are usually smoother than late nights.
💱 Forex — almost all week. Opens Sunday evening, runs to Friday evening ET. Closed most of the weekend.
📢 One rule: if the app shows an earnings warning for a stock that day, either skip it or expect a BIG swing either way.
📖 Kid Dictionary — every trading word in easy words
Call option = A bet that a stock will go UP. Like saying "I think this toy will cost more later!"
Put option = A bet that a stock will go DOWN.
Premium = The price you paid for the bet. If it says $0.35, one bet costs $35 (you always times by 100).
Contract = One full bet. One contract watches 100 shares of the stock.
Strike price = The special number the stock has to pass for your bet to win.
Expiration (Expiry) = The day your bet ends. After this day the bet is over.
Sell limit = You tell the computer "Sell my bet when it reaches THIS price." Then you don't have to watch it — the computer does it for you.
Market order = "Sell it right now!" at whatever price people are paying.
Bid = The most someone will pay you right now. Ask = The least someone will sell to you for right now.
Expected swing (move) = How much the computer thinks the price might jump. Like guessing how far a rubber band will stretch.
Profit = The extra money you make. Gain % = How much bigger your money got. +50% means it grew by half!
Break-even = The price where you don't win OR lose. You just get your money back.
Out of the money (OTM) = Your bet isn't winning YET. In the money (ITM) = Your bet IS winning right now!
Leverage = Your bet can grow way faster than the stock. Like a tiny push that moves a big swing.
Volatility (IV) = How wild and jumpy a stock is. Jumpy = more exciting but more risky.
Max loss = The MOST you can lose. Good news: it's only the money you paid — never more. 🛡️
Conviction = How SURE the computer is about the bet. Higher number = more sure!

Smart trading tricks (good tips from real traders, put in easy words to learn from)
  • Day trade: Opening Range Breakout (ORB) — mark the high/low of the first 5-30 minutes, then trade the breakout with volume confirmation. Repeatedly called one of the cleanest, most reliable day setups.
  • Day trade: VWAP + 9/20 EMA pullbacks — on 5-10 min charts, buy retests in the direction of the trend. Strong on SPY/QQQ, often paired with ORB.
  • Swing: Weekly trend + Optimal Trade Entry (Fib 62-79%) — trade with the weekly trend, enter on the pullback, target ~3R, and close if it takes longer than ~2 weeks.
  • Swing: Momentum breakout after first consolidation — fresh trend with stacked 8/20/50 EMAs, buy the first clean pullback/consolidation breakout.
  • Swing: 200 SMA flip — price breaks above the daily 200 SMA and it turns from resistance into support. Simple continuation.
  • Rule: Specialize in ONE setup and use strict risk (~1% or less per trade). High-signal accounts say this discipline IS the edge — swing traders generally out-earn pure day traders.
